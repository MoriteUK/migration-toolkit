Migration Executive Brief

Select the folder of an AvePoint FLY report export (or its CSV files). The brief needs the Mappings summary and, for the security section, the Site security and SharedLink reports. The large “Site objects” files are not needed and are skipped. Everything is read on this computer; nothing is uploaded.

After the brief appears you can click any text on it to edit the wording. “Save as PDF” opens the print dialog: choose Save as PDF, paper A4.
